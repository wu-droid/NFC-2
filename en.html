ABOUT THE BUILDING

Bosco Verticale, located in Milan’s Porta Nuova district, was designed by Boeri Studio and completed in 2014. The project consists of two residential towers whose projecting balconies integrate trees, shrubs, and perennial plants directly into the façades, creating a vertical ecosystem within a high-density urban setting. The towers rise to approximately 110 metres and 76 metres and host around 800 trees, 4,500 shrubs, and 20,000 plants. Vegetation is distributed according to the solar exposure of each façade, making the project a prominent example of how architecture, urban regeneration, and biodiversity can be combined within the contemporary city.
